# Paper showcase background generation

Tool: built-in Image Generation. Desktop input: user gallery reference image.
Mobile input: the generated desktop background, used as a style reference.
Generated PNGs remain outside Git in the Codex generated-images folder; only
the optimized WebP assets are included in the website.

## Desktop prompt

Edit target: supplied Visol gallery reference. Create ONLY its elegant photographic BACKGROUND PLATE, landscape 1824x1024, for a luxury invitation website. Remove ALL text, lettering, logos, numbers, invitation sheets, crumpled paper balls, cover art, UI icons, and ALL shadows cast by those removed papers. Preserve the warm light ivory matte tabletop / fine paper atmosphere, delicate out-of-focus white blossom branch in the extreme upper RIGHT corner, a few very sparse blush petals at extreme lower corners and a couple peripheral falling petals. Soft realistic late morning sunlight entering from the UPPER RIGHT casts subtle diagonal botanical shadows toward the LOWER LEFT. It must look dimensional, editorial and expensive, pale cream not yellow, no dark regions. Huge open quiet centre and upper-left for live webpage typography and three interactive paper objects that will be rendered separately. Keep botanical ornament mostly in the outer 12% and never clutter the central 75%. No cards, no paper balls, no letters, no text whatsoever. Gentle natural photographic detail, airy fine surface texture, seamlessly light ivory at top and bottom edges. This is a usable empty background asset, not a finished webpage screenshot.

## Mobile prompt

Supporting style reference: supplied ivory botanical gallery background plate. Create a NEW separately art-directed PORTRAIT MOBILE background asset, 1024x1536, for the same luxury invitation website. NOT a crop of the landscape. Photorealistic macro editorial still-life background: pale warm ivory fine matte paper / limestone tabletop, soft champagne sunlight entering from upper RIGHT, delicate soft diagonal botanical shadows travelling toward lower LEFT. A tiny elegant spray of white blossoms cropped at the extreme TOP RIGHT, one softly blurred blush petal near bottom left and two tiny petals at far bottom-right edge; one very subtle sage leaf tip at extreme left midpoint if helpful. The centre must be wide open and quiet for live interactive content: top 25% hosts a headline, middle 40% will host TWO SMALL paper objects side by side, bottom central 25% will host ONE SMALL paper object. Do NOT draw any of these objects, no crumpled paper, no sheets, no printed invitations, no cards, no text, no lettering, no UI or mockup frames, no cast shadows from nonexistent papers. Everything ornamental stays at the edges. Rich realistic gentle light and surface depth, airy dreamy premium but restrained, no dark region, no busy flora, white blending edges. The real site's papers and shadows are separate rendered elements. Output ONLY the clean empty photographic portrait background.
