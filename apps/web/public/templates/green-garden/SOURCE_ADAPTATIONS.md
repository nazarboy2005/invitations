The canonical capture is Chorlove's server-rendered `preview-frame.html` snapshot. Its exact archived bytes are preserved at `source/preview-frame.html`; the source hash and byte length in `source-assets.provenance.json` match the Chorlove archive. The snapshot contains Next.js runtime data and sample invitation content, but the runnable page uses the isolated adapted structure, local shared styles, and Green Garden assets rather than the captured site runtime.

Green Garden's own floral background, opening decoration, source ornaments, and Cormorant Garamond, Qellia, and The Nautigal font files remain packaged with the renderer. Direct comparison against the recovered provider archive confirms all 15 Green Garden image files and all three fonts match their packaged copies byte-for-byte. Their SHA-256 values are recorded in `source-assets.provenance.json` and checked by the focused test. The source program uses three decorative schedule icons: `welcome.webp`, `cake.webp`, and `dish.webp`; none is in the Green Garden archive folder, but their exact Chorlove URLs returned 200 and their packaged copies match the retrieved source bytes. Their hashes are recorded in the manifest. The snapshot's six gallery image URLs point to Orchid Amplop sample photos, so those cross-template sample photos stay excluded. The archived source contains five references to `/images/green-garden/leaf1-bloom.webp`: two in the hero and three along the long page. All five are preserved in the adapted markup. The recovered provider archive has no Green Garden copy; its only same-named file is Glass Pink's different flower asset. The missing source art remains un-substituted.

The Chorlove adapter maps the shared invitation contract to the couple, greeting, event, date/calendar, venue/map, schedule, creator gallery, contacts, RSVP, wishes, To'yona card details, music, locale controls, countdown, and reduced-motion reveal behavior. Preview gallery placeholders come from the shared sample fixture and remain ephemeral. A creator's uploaded gallery images replace those empty preview slots; in public invitations, the optional gallery section stays hidden until an authored image is present. Sample invitation values and cross-template gallery photos are never added to drafts or published invitations.

The catalog artwork is the full 432×768 portrait opening captured from Chorlove's archived source snapshot, including the source names/date, original flower arrangement, opening button, and music toggle. `catalog-cover-opening.png` and `preview.provenance.json` record its capture details and SHA-256; the former packaged-renderer preview remains available as QA evidence.
