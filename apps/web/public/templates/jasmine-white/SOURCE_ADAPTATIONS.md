# Jasmine White source adaptations

The visual reference is `source/preview-frame.html`, captured byte-for-byte from the archived Chorlove Jasmine White page. The original template module and animation definitions are in `Invitations - this folder will be removed from production/Chorlove/Web invitations/_next/static/chunks/0ziyt946s2g41.js` (SHA-256 `C126D27D5F78DA03D1EF1C4FC5320247D00DC18F697CD3EF0CE2E250AE1335C4`). Both captured stylesheets now load byte-for-byte from the isolated package. The capture and copied asset hashes are recorded in `source-assets.provenance.json`.

`bridge.js` keeps the captured HTML, CSS, images, and font files in the package, and binds invitation data and platform actions to those elements. It replays the source envelope fade (600 ms after a 450 ms delay), envelope lift (800 ms), flower exit (800 ms), petal burst (850 ms), and 1,050 ms overlay lifetime. On opening, the hero enters over 900 ms after a 200 ms delay. The opener also has 12 source-configured falling petals, a heart pulse (2 seconds), and button sheen (3 seconds). Section reveal profiles preserve the source transforms, transition timings, delays, and viewport thresholds. The seven gift flowers use the source lift, rotation, duration, and delay values. The gift artwork opens a source-styled full-screen modal with the captured 250 ms backdrop and card entrance/exit; card details and copy action are supplied by the platform adapter.

The captured The Nautigal, Fraunces, Jost, and Marcellus fonts were missing from the runtime font map; the exact archive copies are now packaged with local URLs and hashes. Reduced motion skips continuous decorations and the opening transitions, shows section content immediately, and still allows the envelope and gift modal to open and close. Source sample names and invitation content are replaced by platform data. Source application runtime, analytics, and demo data are not part of the shipped template.
